# Trading Psychology: Mastering Your Emotions

The biggest difference between successful and unsuccessful traders isn't their strategy — it's their **mindset**.

## The Two Enemies of Every Trader

### 1. Fear

Fear manifests in several ways:

- **Fear of missing out (FOMO)**: Entering trades too late
- **Fear of losing**: Closing winning trades too early
- **Fear of being wrong**: Holding losing trades too long

### 2. Greed

Greed leads to:

- **Overleveraging**: Risking too much on a single trade
- **Overtrading**: Taking too many positions
- **Moving stop losses**: Hoping for more profit

## The Trading Plan

> "A goal without a plan is just a wish." — Antoine de Saint-Exupery

Every trader needs:

1. **Entry rules**: When to enter a trade
2. **Exit rules**: When to take profit
3. **Risk rules**: How much to risk per trade
4. **Position sizing**: How large each position should be

## Building Discipline

### Create a Checklist

Before every trade, ask:

- [ ] Does this trade match my strategy?
- [ ] Is my risk within acceptable limits?
- [ ] Do I have a clear exit plan?
- [ ] Am I trading based on analysis, not emotion?

### Keep a Trading Journal

Track every trade with:

```
Date | Pair | Direction | Entry | Stop Loss | Take Profit | Result | Notes
2026-01-15 | EUR/USD | Long | 1.0850 | 1.0820 | 1.0910 | +60 pips | Good setup
```

## Mental Models for Trading

| Emotion | Trigger | Response |
|---------|---------|----------|
| FOMO | Big move without you | Accept you missed it |
| Revenge | After a loss | Take a break |
| Overconfidence | After wins | Stick to your rules |
| Analysis paralysis | Too many signals | Simplify your approach |

## Key Principles

1. **Accept losses** as part of the business
2. **Focus on process**, not profits
3. **Be patient** — wait for A+ setups
4. **Stay humble** — the market can always surprise you
5. **Manage risk** — survival comes first

> "The goal of a successful trader is to make the best trades. Money is secondary." — Alexander Elder
