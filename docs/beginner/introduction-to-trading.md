# Introduction to Trading

Welcome to the world of trading! This lesson will introduce you to the basics of financial markets and how trading works.

## What is Trading?

Trading is the act of buying and selling financial instruments such as stocks, currencies, commodities, or derivatives with the goal of making a profit.

### Types of Trading

| Type | Timeframe | Style |
|------|-----------|-------|
| Scalping | Seconds to minutes | Very short-term |
| Day Trading | Minutes to hours | Intraday positions |
| Swing Trading | Days to weeks | Medium-term |
| Position Trading | Weeks to months | Long-term |

## Key Concepts

### Market Orders

A **market order** is an order to buy or sell immediately at the best available current price.

> "The market can stay irrational longer than you can stay solvent." — John Maynard Keynes

### Limit Orders

A **limit order** sets the maximum price you're willing to pay (for buying) or the minimum price you're willing to accept (for selling).

### Bid and Ask

- **Bid**: The highest price a buyer is willing to pay
- **Ask**: The lowest price a seller is willing to accept
- **Spread**: The difference between bid and ask

## Your First Steps

1. Open a demo account
2. Learn to read charts
3. Understand risk management
4. Start with small positions
5. Keep a trading journal

## Code Example

```python
# Simple moving average calculation
def sma(prices, period):
    return sum(prices[-period:]) / period

prices = [100, 102, 101, 103, 105, 104]
print(f"SMA(3): {sma(prices, 3):.2f}")
```

## Summary
